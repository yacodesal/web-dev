The following movies will be rated by the:

Quality of actors

Ability to make me laugh or cry

Plot

Sense and Sensibility

There are some big actors in this movie who are very good. This movie features: Kate Winslet, Emma Tompson, Hugh Grant, Alan Rickman, Rober Hardy, Hugh Laurie, and Imelda Staunton. This movie did not make me cry, but it almost did. There were a few times where I found myslef laughing out loud because of it. The plot has a some good twists to it, however I found that some scenes of the movie were mundane. Overall, I rate the movie a 3/5.

Avatar: Way of the Water

There are some fun actors in this movie that make it great. This movie features: Zoe Saldana, Sigourney Weaver, Kate Winslet, Sam Worthington, Stephen Lang, and Jack Champion. There were a good amount of times that I had a hard laugh from the characters' dialouge and looks. This movie's plot did make me cry. There is some quality character development that I enjoyed. Overall, I rate this movie a 4/5.

5 Feet Apart

This movie has lesser known actors, but they do a good job at playing their character. The movie features: Haley Lu Richardson, Clare Forlani, Kimberly Herbert Gregory, Cole Sprouse, and Moises Arias. Two character made me laugh some, but I wouldn't say that the movie as a whole is really funny. I did cry like a baby at one point though. The plot is fun, and it has sweet characters, but it's not what you expect at first. Overall, I rate the movie a 3/5.

Mamma Mia! Here We Go Again

This movie has AMAZING actors. It features: Meryl Streep, Amanda Seyfried, Julie Walters, Christine Baranski, Stellan Skarsgard, Pierce Brosnan, Colin Firth, and Dominic Cooper. The characters are really fun, they made me laugh so much that I cried. The plot is captivating, and keeps you on the edge of your seat. Overall, I rate athe movie a 5/5.

I Still Believe

The actors in this movie are not well known, and for a reason. They're not horrible actors, but there is something missing, a je ne sais pas. The main actors are: Britt Robertson, KJ Apa, and Nathan Parsons. It is a sweet movie that had me at some laughing moments, and moments where I cried a river. The plot is not something crazy adventurous or anything like that, but it is enjoyable to watch. Overall, I rate this movie a 2/5.
